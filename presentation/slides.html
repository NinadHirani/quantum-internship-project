The Problem: Capacitated Vehicle Routing (CVRP)

Given a depot, n customers with demands, and K vehicles with capacity Q:

  • Find routes starting/ending at the depot
  • Visit every customer exactly once
  • Respect vehicle capacity constraints
  • Minimize total distance

NP-Hard: CVRP generalizes TSP — solution space grows combinatorially

Our Instance

  • 1 depot + 5 customers
  • 2 vehicles, capacity Q = 15
  • Demands: [2, 1, 6, 4, 4]
  • Total demand = 17 (must split across vehicles)
  • Seed = 42 (fully reproducible)

Why Quantum Optimization?

Classical Approach

  • OR-Tools — Google's optimization library
  • Metaheuristic: Guided Local Search
  • Fast, practical, industry-standard
  • No optimality guarantee (but excellent in practice)

Quantum Approach

  • QAOA — Quantum Approximate Optimization
  • Hybrid quantum-classical loop
  • Encodes cost function as a Hamiltonian
  • Explores solution space via superposition

Research question: How does QAOA compare to classical heuristics on a small, well-defined CVRP instance?

Convert CVRP → Quadratic Unconstrained Binary Optimization:

Position-Based Encoding

Binary variable x[i,p] = 1 if customer i is at position p in the route sequence.

minimize   xTQx  =  [distance objective] + λ₁·(coverage penalty)² + λ₂·(capacity penalty)²

Constraints → Penalties

  • Coverage: each customer visited exactly once
  • Position: each position occupied by one customer
  • Capacity: vehicle load ≤ Q

Scale

  • 5 customers × 5 positions = 25 binary vars
  • + 8 auxiliary (slack) vars = 33 qubits total
  • Penalty weights: λ ≈ 450 (heuristic)

Discussion & Key Findings

🏆 Classical Wins (for now)

  • OR-Tools finds near-optimal solutions instantly
  • Industry-proven, reliable, scalable
  • Handles constraints natively (no penalty tuning)

⚡ Quantum Challenges

  • Qubit overhead: 5 customers → 33 qubits
  • Penalty sensitivity: λ too low → infeasible; too high → poor optimization
  • Simulation cost: exponential in qubit count

Honest Assessment

QAOA does not outperform classical heuristics at this scale — and that's an expected and valid research finding. The value lies in demonstrating the full quantum optimization pipeline and understanding where the bottlenecks are.

Limitations & Future Work

Current Limitations

  • Simulator only — no real quantum hardware
  • Small instance (5 customers) due to qubit ceiling
  • No noise modeling (ideal simulation)
  • QAOA limited to p = 1–2 layers (deeper = too slow)
  • Position encoding is qubit-hungry: O(n²)

Future Directions

  • 🔮 Run on real QPU (IBM Quantum / AWS Braket)
  • 📈 Larger instances with hybrid decomposition
  • 🧪 Warm-start QAOA (initialize from classical solution)
  • ⏰ Time-window extension (VRPTW)
  • 🔄 Recursive QAOA (RQAOA) for better quality
  • 📊 Noise-aware benchmarking

Conclusion

We built a complete, reproducible pipeline comparing classical and quantum optimization for CVRP.

While QAOA doesn't yet outperform classical heuristics at this scale, this project demonstrates the full end-to-end quantum optimization workflow — from mathematical formulation to QUBO to Ising to QAOA — and provides an honest benchmark for future comparison.
